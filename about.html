Research-Driven & Universal

Democratizing Acoustic Storytelling for All Audiences

StoryCast was founded on a simple conviction: storytelling should never exclude anyone due to sensory disability, device limitations, or neurodiversity. We blend investigative journalism, pristine field acoustics, and uncompromising accessibility standards.

Our Triad of Universal Storytelling

Traditional podcasts and video documentaries frequently leave behind deaf, blind, or motor-impaired audiences. StoryCast eliminates these barriers through three foundational pillars:

1. Pristine Acoustic Engineering

All audio tracks are mastered to -16 LUFS with controlled dynamic range, ensuring crystal-clear speech intelligibility even against rich background ambient soundscapes.

2. Verbatim Synchronized Transcripts

Transcripts are not an afterthought. Every story includes time-coded cues, descriptive sound effects, speaker identifications, and clickable seeking for non-linear consumption.

3. Semantic & Container-Responsive Web

Shipped purely in semantic HTML5 and modern Sass without JavaScript framework overhead. Content elements adapt gracefully to available container dimensions via CSS Container Queries.
