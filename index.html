How StoryCast Prioritizes Universal Access

Every story is engineered to remove barriers for blind, low-vision, deaf, hard-of-hearing, and neurodivergent audiences.

Synchronized Transcripts

Read along with highlighted speaker tags and audio cue descriptions. Click any timestamp to jump the player straight to that moment.

Closed Captions & Track Data

Standard WebVTT captions embedded with <track> elements describe environmental sounds, musical cues, and dialogue tone.

Keyboard & Screen Reader First

High-visibility 3px focus rings, logical tab order, ARIA live-regions for player updates, and dedicated skip links on every page.
