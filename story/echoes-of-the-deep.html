Field Research & Acoustic Context

At three kilometers below sea level, sunlight is entirely extinguished. The bathypelagic zone represents the largest living habitat on Earth, yet it remains one of the least mapped. In this realm of eternal shadow, vision is of little consequence. Sound travels almost four and a half times faster through saltwater than through air, making acoustic waves the primary sensory medium of life.

During the October 2026 expedition of the research vessel Acoustic Explorer, our team deployed calibrated hydrophones sensitive from 5 Hertz to 120 kilohertz. What we captured was not a silent abyss, but an uninterrupted biological conversation stretching across entire ocean basins.

Technical Equipment Specifications & Hydrophone Calibration
  • Acoustic Transducer: Reson TC4032 Low-Noise Spherical Hydrophone.
  • Usable Frequency Band: 5 Hz to 120,000 Hz (±2 dB).
  • Sampling Rate: 192 kHz / 24-bit floating point ADC.
  • Depth Rating: Titanium pressure vessel rated to 6,000 meters.
  • Sound Pressure Calibration: Normalized to 1 μPa reference level.
